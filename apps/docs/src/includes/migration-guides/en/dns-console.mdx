To create the zone in Azion Console:

<DocSteps>
  <DocStep title="Open Edge DNS">

  Access [Azion Console](https://console.azion.com/) > **Edge DNS**.

  </DocStep>
  <DocStep title="Select + Zone" />
  <DocStep title="Enter the zone">

  Enter a **Name** for the zone, and the **Domain Name**. The domain cannot change after the zone is created.

  </DocStep>
  <DocStep title="(Optional) Turn on DNSSEC">

  Under **DNSSEC**, turn on **Enable DNSSEC**.

  </DocStep>
  <DocStep title="Select Save" />
  <DocStep title="Add the records">

  In the **Records** tab, create each record of the current zone.

  </DocStep>
</DocSteps>

The zone answers on the Azion nameservers. Then change the nameservers of the domain at the registrar. For the steps, refer to [Create, edit, or delete a zone](/en/documentation/guides/application-security/dns/edge-dns-configure-main-settings/) and [Add, edit, or delete a record](/en/documentation/guides/application-security/dns/add-records/).
