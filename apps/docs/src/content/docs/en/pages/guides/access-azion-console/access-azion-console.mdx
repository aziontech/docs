You can create an Azion account, sign in to [Azion Console](https://console.azion.com/), and reach the account pages from the account menu. For what each area of the Console holds once you are in, such as the products menu and the homepage shortcuts, refer to [About Azion Console](/en/documentation/guides/platform/account-and-billing/getting-to-know-azion-console/).

---

## Prerequisites

- An email address to register with Azion.
- For a user with multi-factor authentication (MFA) turned on, the phone that runs the authenticator application linked to that user.

---

## Create an account

Azion Console signs you in with the email and the password you register at sign-up.

To create an account, open the [sign-up page](https://console.azion.com/signup/) and register your email address and a password. For more information, refer to [Create an account](/en/documentation/fundamentals/creating-account/).

You can now sign in with those credentials.

---

## Sign in

Each session starts from the sign-in page of the Console, with the credentials of your account.

To sign in to Azion Console:

<DocSteps>
  <DocStep title="Open Azion Console">

  Access [Azion Console](https://console.azion.com/).

  </DocStep>
  <DocStep title="Enter your credentials">

  Enter the email and the password you registered for the account.

  </DocStep>
  <DocStep title="Select Sign In" />
</DocSteps>

The Console homepage opens. If MFA is on for your user, the Console asks for the six-digit code from the authenticator application first.

:::tip
To protect the account with a second factor at each sign-in, turn on [multi-factor authentication](/en/documentation/fundamentals/multi-factor-authentication/). For the procedure, refer to [Enable multi-factor authentication](/en/documentation/guides/platform/account-and-billing/multi-factor-authentication/).
:::

---

## Open an account page

The account menu opens from the Console header. It holds the pages for the account, its users, its billing, and your own profile.

To open an account page:

<DocSteps>
  <DocStep title="Open the account menu">

  Access [Azion Console](https://console.azion.com/) and open the account menu.

  </DocStep>
  <DocStep title="Select the page">

  For example, select **Account Settings** for the data of the account, or **Your Settings** for your own profile.

  </DocStep>
</DocSteps>

The page you selected opens. To find every entry of the account menu, refer to [About Azion Console](/en/documentation/guides/platform/account-and-billing/getting-to-know-azion-console/#account-menu).

---

## Change the theme

The account menu also sets the color theme of the Console.

To change the theme:

<DocSteps>
  <DocStep title="Open the account menu">

  Access [Azion Console](https://console.azion.com/) and open the account menu.

  </DocStep>
  <DocStep title="Select the theme">

  Under **Theme**, select _Light_, _Dark_, or _System_.

  </DocStep>
</DocSteps>

The Console renders in the theme you selected.

---

## Sign out

The account menu also ends your session.

To sign out of Azion Console:

<DocSteps>
  <DocStep title="Open the account menu">

  Access [Azion Console](https://console.azion.com/) and open the account menu.

  </DocStep>
  <DocStep title="Select Logout" />
</DocSteps>

The session ends. To use the Console again, sign in.
