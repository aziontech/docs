You can delete an Azion account from **Account Settings** in Azion Console. To change the name, company, or address of the account and keep it, refer to [Configure account settings](/en/documentation/guides/platform/account-and-billing/account-settings/).

---

## Prerequisites

- Access to Azion Console as the **Account Owner**. The **Delete account** button shows only to a user with Account Owner privileges.
- No pending debts on the account. To review them, refer to [Billing](/en/documentation/guides/platform/account-and-billing/billing-and-subscriptions/).

---

## Delete the account

Before the deletion runs, a window lists the status of items in the account, such as **Debts**. Any debt it shows must be cleared before you can finish.

:::caution
Deleting an account is permanent. The confirmation states that the action removes the Personal Account and all associated data from Azion's platform, and that it cannot be undone.
:::

To delete the account in Azion Console:

<DocSteps>
  <DocStep title="Open Account Settings">

  Access [Azion Console](https://console.azion.com/) and, in the account menu, select **Account Settings**.

  </DocStep>
  <DocStep title="Start the deletion">

  In the **Danger Zone** section, select **Delete account**.

  </DocStep>
  <DocStep title="Confirm the deletion">

  In the text box, enter `DELETE`.

  </DocStep>
  <DocStep title="Select Delete account" />
</DocSteps>

Azion Console shows *Your account has been deleted* and opens the [sign-up page](https://console.azion.com/signup/). An email confirming the deletion reaches the address of the account. To tell Azion about your experience, write to [feedback@azion.com](mailto:feedback@azion.com).
