You can review the charges of your usage-based plan, manage payment methods, and add credit in **Billing**, from Azion Console. Billing has two tabs: **Bills** and **Payment Methods**. For what each charge and billing field means, refer to the [Billing reference](/en/documentation/fundamentals/billing-and-subscriptions/).

---

## Prerequisites

- Access to Azion Console. To sign in, refer to [Access Azion Console](/en/documentation/guides/platform/account-and-billing/how-to-access-azion-console/).
- An account address, set in the **Address Information** section of **Account Settings**. Billing does not add a payment method without it. To set the address, refer to [Configure your account](/en/documentation/guides/platform/account-and-billing/account-settings/).

---

## Review the current invoice

The **Bills** tab shows the **Current Invoice**, with the billing period and every charge on the account. The same tab shows **Support**, with the active support plan, the payment method, the currency, and the credit balance. Billing also names the **Current Plan**, such as *Hobby Plan* or *Pro Plan*. To switch plans, select **Change Plan**.

To review the current invoice in Azion Console:

<DocSteps>
  <DocStep title="Open Billing">

  Access [Azion Console](https://console.azion.com/) and, in the account menu, select **Billing**.

  </DocStep>
  <DocStep title="Go to the Bills tab" />
  <DocStep title="Open the invoice details">

  In **Current Invoice**, select **Details**.

  </DocStep>
</DocSteps>

**Invoice details** opens and explains each charge of the current invoice.

## Review the payment history

**Payment History** sits in the **Bills** tab. It lists past charges with details such as the payment date, the transactions amount, the payment method, and the invoice, and you can filter them.

To review the payment history in Azion Console:

<DocSteps>
  <DocStep title="Open Billing">

  Access [Azion Console](https://console.azion.com/) and, in the account menu, select **Billing**.

  </DocStep>
  <DocStep title="Go to the Bills tab" />
  <DocStep title="Filter the charges">

  In **Payment History**, filter the list down to the charges you want to inspect.

  </DocStep>
  <DocStep title="Download an invoice">

  (Optional) Select **Download Invoice** for a charge. The download starts as soon as the PDF is ready.

  </DocStep>
</DocSteps>

The list shows only the charges that match the filter. Billing reports each payment with one of these statuses: *Paid*, *Pending*, *Processing*, *Failed*, *Refunded*, *Void*, *Uncollectible*, *Partially Paid*, *Disputed*, *Open*, or *Not charged*.

## Add a payment method

The **Payment Methods** tab lists every payment method linked to the account. Azion requires at least one credit card to use the platform.

To add a credit card in Azion Console:

<DocSteps>
  <DocStep title="Open Billing">

  Access [Azion Console](https://console.azion.com/) and, in the account menu, select **Billing**.

  </DocStep>
  <DocStep title="Go to the Payment Methods tab" />
  <DocStep title="Select Add Payment Method" />
  <DocStep title="Enter the card holder">

  In **Card Holder Name**, enter the name of the credit card owner.

  </DocStep>
  <DocStep title="Enter the card number">

  In **Card Number**, enter the number of the credit card.

  </DocStep>
  <DocStep title="Enter the expiration date">

  In **Expiration Date**, enter the expiration date printed on the card.

  </DocStep>
  <DocStep title="Enter the security code">

  In **Security code (CVC/CVV)**, enter the code printed on the card.

  </DocStep>
  <DocStep title="Select Save" />
</DocSteps>

The card appears in the **Payment Methods** list.

---

## Set the default payment method

Azion charges future transactions to the default payment method automatically.

To set the default payment method in Azion Console:

<DocSteps>
  <DocStep title="Open Billing">

  Access [Azion Console](https://console.azion.com/) and, in the account menu, select **Billing**.

  </DocStep>
  <DocStep title="Go to the Payment Methods tab" />
  <DocStep title="Open the actions of the card">

  In the row of the payment method, open the actions menu.

  </DocStep>
  <DocStep title="Select Set as default" />
</DocSteps>

Future transactions go to that payment method.

---

## Add credit to the account

You can also put a specific amount of credit into the account. In the **Payment Methods** tab of **Billing**, select **Add Credit** and enter the amount in **Credit Amount**. The account's credit balance is listed under **Support**, next to the active support plan, the payment method, and the currency.
