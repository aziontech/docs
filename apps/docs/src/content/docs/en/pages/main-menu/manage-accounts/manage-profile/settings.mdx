You can edit the data and the sign-in options of an Azion account in **Account Settings**, from Azion Console. Your own name, email, and password belong to your user, not to the account: they are on the page described in [Your Settings](/en/documentation/fundamentals/your-settings/).

---

## Prerequisites

- Access to Azion Console. To sign in, refer to [Access Azion Console](/en/documentation/guides/platform/account-and-billing/how-to-access-azion-console/).
- The full address of the account, because the **Address Information** section saves only with every field filled in.

---

## Sections of Account Settings

**Account Settings** groups the account data in six sections:

| Section | Holds |
| --- | --- |
| **General** | **Account Name**, and the ID of the account. |
| **Company Information** | **Company Name**, **Company ID**, and **Billing Emails**. |
| **Address Information** | **Postal Code**, **Country**, **State/Region**, **City**, **Address**, and **Apartment, floor, etc.** |
| **Login Settings** | **Allow Social Login** and **Enforce Multi-Factor Authentication**, which apply to every user of the account. |
| **GitHub Integration** | The connection to your GitHub account, with a **Remove** button. |
| **Danger Zone** | **Delete account**. For the procedure, refer to [Delete your account](/en/documentation/guides/platform/account-and-billing/delete-account/). |

---

## Edit the account data

The **General**, **Company Information**, and **Address Information** sections carry the name, the company, and the address of the account.

To edit the account data in Azion Console:

<DocSteps>
  <DocStep title="Open Account Settings">

  Access [Azion Console](https://console.azion.com/) and, in the account menu, select **Account Settings**.

  </DocStep>
  <DocStep title="Edit the fields">

  In **General**, **Company Information**, or **Address Information**, change the values you need.

  </DocStep>
  <DocStep title="Select Save" />
</DocSteps>

**Account Settings** shows the new values.

:::note
Every field of **Address Information** is mandatory. With one of them empty, the changes cannot be saved.
:::

---

## Turn on sign-in options for every user

The **Login Settings** section sets how every user linked to the account signs in:

- **Allow Social Login** lets those users sign in with the credentials of a social network.
- **Enforce Multi-Factor Authentication** requires MFA at sign-in for all of them.

To turn on a sign-in option in Azion Console:

<DocSteps>
  <DocStep title="Open Account Settings">

  Access [Azion Console](https://console.azion.com/) and, in the account menu, select **Account Settings**.

  </DocStep>
  <DocStep title="Turn on the option">

  In the **Login Settings** section, turn on **Allow Social Login**, **Enforce Multi-Factor Authentication**, or both.

  </DocStep>
  <DocStep title="Select Save" />
</DocSteps>

The option applies to every user linked to the account. For what users see once MFA is enforced, refer to [Enable multi-factor authentication](/en/documentation/guides/platform/account-and-billing/multi-factor-authentication/).
