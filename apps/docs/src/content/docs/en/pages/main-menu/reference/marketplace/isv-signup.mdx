You become a seller on [Azion Marketplace](/en/documentation/platform/marketplace/) by applying as an Independent Software Vendor (ISV) and registering your product through two forms that the Azion team reviews. For who can sell and what it costs, refer to [Seller requirements and fees](/en/documentation/platform/marketplace/marketplace-seller-guide/).

---

## Prerequisites

- An [Azion account](/en/documentation/fundamentals/creating-account/).
- To list a product with an associated charge, your organization must be able to receive payments from Azion, which requires both of these:
  - Legal status in the United States.
  - A bank account based in the United States.

:::caution
Azion Marketplace does not accept third-party companies that resell products.
:::

## Apply as an ISV

The ISV application form collects key information about your organization and your agreement to the [Marketplace Agreement](/en/documentation/agreements/marketplace-agreement/). Azion can also ask you for more information to verify your identity. For more information, refer to [Sign-up](/en/documentation/platform/marketplace/marketplace-seller-guide/#sign-up).

To apply as an ISV:

<DocSteps>
  <DocStep title="Open the application form">

  Go to the [ISV application form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLScrOc_FyjVC11VFbVgDjuh6THh2jJs0-I8vduW0nZSCDGEPIg/viewform).

  </DocStep>
  <DocStep title="Fill in the fields">

  Enter the details of your organization. The form marks each required field with an asterisk.

  </DocStep>
  <DocStep title="Agree to the Marketplace Agreement">

  Read the agreement, then select the **Azion Marketplace Agreement** checkbox.

  </DocStep>
  <DocStep title="(Optional) Request a copy of your answers">

  Turn on **Send me a copy of my responses**. The form sends a copy of your application to the email that you entered.

  </DocStep>
  <DocStep title="Select Submit" />
</DocSteps>

The Azion team receives your ISV application.

The ISV application form has these fields:

| Field | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Email** | Yes | The email that Azion uses to contact you. Customers do not see it when your product is published. |
| **Contact Name** | No | The person responsible for the contract with Azion. Customers do not see it when your product is published. |
| **Company Name or Developer Name** | Yes | The creator of your product. Azion Marketplace shows it when your product is published. |
| **Website** | No | Your website. Azion Marketplace shows it when your product is published. |
| **Company Logo** | No | A URL to the logo file that Azion Marketplace shows when your product is published. The file is `JPG` or `PNG`, with a maximum width of `120px`. |
| **Phone** | No | The phone number that Azion uses to contact you. Customers do not see it. |
| **Azion Marketplace Agreement** | Yes | The checkbox that confirms you read the [Marketplace Agreement](/en/documentation/agreements/marketplace-agreement/) and agree to its terms. |

## Register your product

The product registration form collects the details that Azion needs to publish your product on Azion Marketplace. Register the product after you apply as an ISV.

To register your product:

<DocSteps>
  <DocStep title="Open the registration form">

  Go to the [product registration form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSeoTbF2Qifr7qqHjoaxv9ujFB1Ak4xg6wqV0WfZfpBrpkQ12A/viewform).

  </DocStep>
  <DocStep title="Fill in the ISV information section" />
  <DocStep title="Fill in the product details section" />
  <DocStep title="Fill in the product description section" />
  <DocStep title="Select Submit" />
</DocSteps>

The Azion team receives the registration of your product.

The form marks each required field with an asterisk. Its fields are grouped in three sections.

### ISV information

The ISV information section identifies you as the vendor responsible for the product:

| Field | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Email** | Yes | The email that Azion uses to contact you as the person responsible for the product. |
| **ISV Name** | Yes | Your name as the ISV and creator of the product. Use the same name that you entered in **Company Name or Developer Name** on the ISV application form. |

### Product details

The product details section sets the basic information that the product page on Azion Marketplace shows:

| Field | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Product Name** | Yes | The name that Azion Marketplace shows on the product card and on the product detail page. |
| **Product Version** | Yes | The version of the product that Azion Marketplace makes available. For example: `1.1.0`. |
| **Category** | Yes | The Azion Marketplace category that fits your product best. Select at least one option. |
| **Pricing model** | Yes | The pricing model that fits your product best: _Free_ or _Bring your own license_. For more information, refer to [Seller requirements and fees](/en/documentation/platform/marketplace/marketplace-seller-guide/#business-models). |

### Product description

The product description section holds the text that helps customers understand your product on its Azion Marketplace page:

| Field | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Headline** | Yes | A summary of your product, with a maximum of 80 characters. |
| **Overview** | Yes | A description of your product, with a maximum of 4,000 characters. Explain its purpose and the use cases it solves, and add links to more content, such as documentation, videos, and websites. |
| **Usage information** | Yes | How to use your product, with its requirements and first steps. Add more content as needed, such as documentation, webinars, how-to guides, user agreements, and Terms of Service. |
| **Support** | Yes | The support available for this product, with your service channels. |

---

## Wait for approval

After you submit both forms, the Azion team reviews your ISV application and your product registration. The review checks that both are compliant and that your product is a good fit for Azion Marketplace.

The Azion team confirms the approval by the email that you registered. The same email explains the remaining steps to publish your product on Azion Marketplace.
