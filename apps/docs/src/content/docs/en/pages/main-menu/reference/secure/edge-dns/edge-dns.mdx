**Edge DNS** is a Platform Resource that hosts the DNS records of your domains and answers the queries for them, on Azion's distributed infrastructure. An [authoritative DNS](https://www.azion.com/en/learning/dns/what-is-authoritative-dns/) service holds a domain's records, such as its website's address, and gives the final answer to each lookup for them. Use Edge DNS to point your domains at your servers or a [Workload](/en/documentation/platform/workloads/), set up records for your email services, or prove domain ownership.

Edge DNS works with these entities and settings:

- [Zones and records](/en/documentation/platform/edge-dns/zones-and-records/) hold what Edge DNS answers: a zone for each domain you host, holding the records of that domain's names. A domain can live in only one zone across every Azion account.
- [Record types](/en/documentation/platform/edge-dns/record-types/) set what a record answers, such as an address, another hostname, a mail server, or a text value. For example, an ANAME record points the apex, the zone's domain itself, at a Workload, where a CNAME record is refused.
- [DNSSEC](/en/documentation/platform/edge-dns/dnssec/) lets a zone sign its answers, so a validating resolver can tell when an answer was changed on its way to the client. Resolvers validate the signatures once your registrar publishes the delegation signer (DS) record that Azion generates for the zone.

## Query path

Creating a zone sends it no lookup: a domain's lookups reach its zone only after the domain's registrar delegates the domain to Edge DNS. Before then, you can build the zone and check its answers by querying an Edge DNS nameserver directly, as [Query a zone with dig](/en/documentation/guides/application-security/dns/run-the-dig-command/) shows.

The diagram follows a lookup of `www.example.com`. Solid arrows carry the query, and dotted arrows carry the answer. Each label starts with the number of its step below.

```mermaid

flowchart TD
  Client(["Client"])
  Resolver[["Resolver"]]
  TLD[["TLD nameservers"]]
  NS["Edge DNS nameserver"]
  Zone["Edge DNS: zone"]
  Record["Edge DNS: record"]
  Client -->|"1. lookup"| Resolver
  Resolver -->|"2. who answers?"| TLD
  TLD -.->|"2. Edge DNS nameservers"| Resolver
  Resolver -->|"3. query"| NS
  NS -->|"4. domain"| Zone
  Zone -->|"4. name and type"| Record
  NS -.->|"5. authoritative answer"| Resolver
  NS -.->|"6. NXDOMAIN or REFUSED"| Resolver
  Resolver -.->|"5, 6. answer"| Client
```

1. A client asks its own resolver, not Edge DNS, for `www.example.com`.
2. The resolver asks the nameservers of the top-level domain (TLD), here `.com`, which nameservers answer for `example.com`. They return the three nameservers that the registrar set: `ns1.aziondns.net`, `ns2.aziondns.com`, and `ns3.aziondns.org`.
3. The resolver sends the query to one of the three Edge DNS nameservers.
4. The nameserver selects the zone whose domain matches the query, then the record whose name and type match it.
5. The nameserver returns the record's values as an authoritative answer. The resolver keeps that answer for its time to live (TTL) and passes it to the client.
6. When no record matches the name, the nameserver answers `NXDOMAIN`. When the zone is inactive, or no zone holds the domain, it answers `REFUSED`.
