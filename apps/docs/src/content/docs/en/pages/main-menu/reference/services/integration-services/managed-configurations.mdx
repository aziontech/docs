The **Managed Configurations** service provides management and control over the Platform Resources in your Azion account. This service employs the expertise of a team of experienced engineers to tackle certain day-to-day tasks on your behalf, such as setting up and maintaining your configurations.

The Managed Configuration service is operated by **Solution Architects**, who are responsible for implementing configuration changes requested by you, as the Azion customer. To request the Managed Configuration service for applications running on Azion, [open a Support Ticket](https://tickets.azion.com/en/support/login). 

:::note
This service is a monthly package of contracted hours at a fixed price. Hours not used in a month do not roll over to the next month. See [Pricing](/en/documentation/fundamentals/pricing/) for more information on how this service incurs charges.
:::

## Out-of-scope activities

- **Technical support**: all [technical support tickets](/en/documentation/support/open-tickets/) must be opened through Azion's official support channels and will be assigned to the appropriate team.
- **Support to other providers**: this service does not provide support to any infrastructure and applications that are not running on Azion or through Azion Marketplace integrations provided by third-party vendors.
- **Mitigate workload failures or DDoS attacks**: the [Security Response Team](/en/documentation/services/security-response-team/) provides the service to mitigate workload failures and DDoS attacks.
- **Security audits**: this service does not perform security audits. Azion provides compliance support for Azion's products, such as **SOC 2/3**, **PCI**, and **Global Infrastructure Custom Maps**.
- **Manage end-user devices**: this service does not manage end-user devices, such as laptops, desktops, or smartphones.
