The **Account Settings** page manages the information and the security settings of your account. Azion Console shows it in six sections: **General**, **Company Information**, **Address Information**, **Login Settings**, **GitHub Integration**, and **Danger Zone**, which holds the account deletion.

If you want to manage your personal information, open the **Account menu**, on the top-right corner, and select the **Your Settings** option.

:::note
Only the *Account Owner* can edit **Login Settings** and delete the account.
:::

---

## Account ID and name

The **General** section shows the identity of the account on Azion:

- **ID**: the ID of your account on Azion. It cannot be changed. Use it to open support tickets about the account.

- **Account Name** (mandatory): your account name. You can modify the name as you wish.

---

## Company information

This section holds the data of the company associated with the account:

- **Company Name**: the name of the company associated with the account.

- **Company ID**: a personal or company ID number that identifies who owns the account, such as a tax ID or a company registration number.

- **Billing Emails**: email address to receive the billing emails. You can inform one or more email addresses. Each email should be separated by a semicolon(;) and you can type up to 256 characters.

---

## Address information

All the fields in this section are mandatory, except **Apartment, floor, etc.** Once you change your information in the **Account Settings** page, you will have to fill in all the mandatory fields, including **Address information**. Otherwise, you will not be able to save your changes. 

The address information fields are related to the billing account information:

- **Country:** country of the billing account.
- **State/Region**: state/region of the billing account.
- **City**: city of the billing account.
- **Address**: address of the billing account.
- **Apartment, floor, etc.** (optional): additional information for the address, such as the floor.
- **Postal code**: postal code of the billing account.

## Login settings

### Social Login

If you are the *Account Owner*, you can enable and disable the **Social Login** functionality using its switch. When it is enabled, users linked to the account can authenticate to Azion's platform using the supported social networks. Otherwise, they have to log in using their email and password. Learn more about it on the [Social Login](/en/documentation/fundamentals/social-login/) page.

:::note
When you enable **Social Login**, it applies to all users of the account.
:::

Once Social Login is enabled, users can access Azion via Social Login on their next login.

Azion supports Social Login with GitHub and Google accounts.

### Multi-Factor Authentication

**Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)** provides an extra level of security to protect your users' credentials. Only the *Account Owner* can enable these settings. 

:::note
When MFA is enabled, it will be mandatory for all users of the account, except the **Account owner**.
:::

Learn how to set up an MFA mobile application on the [Multi-Factor Authentication](/en/documentation/fundamentals/multi-factor-authentication/) page.

## GitHub integration

This section connects your GitHub account to Azion, which enables the repository integration and deployment features, such as [importing a project from GitHub](/en/documentation/guides/application-development/automation/import-an-existing-project-from-github/). Each connected GitHub account is listed as a **Connected Account** with the **Integration active** status and a **Remove** button, which disconnects that account.

---

## Delete account

Azion's platform allows you to delete your account. 

Only the *Account Owner* can delete the account. If you have *Account Owner* privileges, you will see the **Delete account** button in the **Danger Zone** section, at the bottom of the page.

After successfully deleting your account, you will receive an email confirming the deletion of your account. If you want to give feedback on your experience with Azion, contact the team through [this email](mailto:feedback@azion.com).

:::note
Your account information is stored for future conferences.
:::

You can [create a new account](/en/documentation/fundamentals/creating-account/) at any time and continue your experience with Azion.
