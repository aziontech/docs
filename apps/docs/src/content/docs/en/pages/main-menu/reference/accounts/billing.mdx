The Billing page provides a comprehensive view of your usage-based pricing model. It is where you see your charges based on how much you use Azion products.

---

## Plans and included usage

Every account is on one of three plans: Hobby, Pro, or Enterprise. Each plan includes an amount of usage for billing metrics such as requests, data transfer, storage, and compute time. Usage within the included amount has no additional charge. What happens to usage beyond it depends on the plan.

For the included usage of each plan and the price of each billing metric, refer to [Pricing](/en/documentation/fundamentals/pricing/).

---

## Real-Time Info and Precise Billing

When comparing the data displayed on Real-Time Metrics and Azion Billing data, you may find differences.

Real-Time Metrics focuses on performance, and therefore uses an at-most-once approach, meaning data may be considered one time or may not be considered at all. Billing, on the other hand, seeks precisions, and therefore uses an exactly-once approach, meaning data is considered precisely one time. You should always consider Azion Billing data as the correct one if you encounter differences.

On average, the difference between the two is smaller than 1%.
