The Billing page provides a comprehensive view of your usage-based pricing model. It is where you see your charges based on how much you use Azion products.

You can visit the [pricing](https://www.azion.com/en/pricing/) page to check prices for Azion products and services and Savings Plans.

---

## Plans

Azion offers three plans: Hobby, which is free, Pro, and Enterprise. Each plan includes an amount of usage and sets resource limits. What happens above the included usage depends on the plan:

- **Hobby**: you may need to upgrade your plan, or Azion may restrict your account.
- **Pro**: Azion bills the additional usage at on-demand prices, and you can set spend limits to control it.
- **Enterprise**: Azion bills the additional usage at on-demand prices. You can also pay entirely on demand, with no fixed commitment, or reduce costs with Reserved Capacity or a Savings Plan.

Pro is billed monthly or annually. An upgrade takes effect immediately, and a downgrade or a cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing cycle. For the prices of each product, refer to [Pricing](/en/documentation/fundamentals/pricing/).

## Real-Time Info and Precise Billing

When comparing the data displayed on Real-Time Metrics and Azion Billing data, you may find differences.

Real-Time Metrics focuses on performance, and therefore uses an at-most-once approach, meaning data may be considered one time or may not be considered at all. Billing, on the other hand, seeks precisions, and therefore uses an exactly-once approach, meaning data is considered precisely one time. You should always consider Azion Billing data as the correct one if you encounter differences.

On average, the difference between the two is smaller than 1%.
