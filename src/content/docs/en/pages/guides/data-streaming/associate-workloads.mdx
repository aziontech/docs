You can associate [workloads](/en/documentation/platform/workloads/) with a [Data Stream](/en/documentation/platform/data-stream/) stream from Azion Console. To associate domains with a stream instead, refer to [How to associate domains on Data Stream](/en/documentation/guides/platform/observability/data-stream-associate-domains/).

A stream collects the events of the workloads associated with it and sends them to the endpoint you configure on the stream. One stream can collect the events of a single workload or of several. You choose them in the **Workloads** section of the stream.

---

## Prerequisites

- One or more workloads on your account. To create a workload, refer to [Workloads quickstart](/en/documentation/platform/workloads/quickstart/).
- Access to Azion Console. For more information, refer to [How to access Azion Console](/en/documentation/guides/platform/account-and-billing/how-to-access-azion-console/).

---

## Create a stream for your workloads

You choose the workloads in the same form that names the stream and sets its source and template. The choice is either every workload on your account or a list you build.

To create the stream in Azion Console:

<DocSteps>
  <DocStep title="Open the Data Stream page">

  Access [Azion Console](https://console.azion.com/) > **Observe** > **Data Stream**.

  </DocStep>
  <DocStep title="Select + Stream" />
  <DocStep title="Enter a name for the stream" />
  <DocStep title="Select the source">

  In **Source**, select the data source of the events. For more information, refer to [Data sources](/en/documentation/platform/data-stream/#data-sources).

  </DocStep>
  <DocStep title="Select the template">

  In **Template**, select the template for the events. For more information, refer to [Templates](/en/documentation/platform/data-stream/#templates).

  </DocStep>
  <DocStep title="Choose the workload option">

  In **Workloads** > **Option**, select one of these values:

  - _All Current and Future Workloads_: Azion selects every workload on your account, including the workloads you create later.
  - _Filter Workloads_: the stream collects only the workloads you move to **Chosen Workloads**.

  </DocStep>
  <DocStep title="Move the workloads to Chosen Workloads">

  If you selected _Filter Workloads_, use the controls of the **Available Workloads** list. Otherwise, skip this step.

  - To find a workload, enter characters or words, or scroll through the list.
  - To move a workload to **Chosen Workloads**, select it and then select `>`.
  - To move several at once, select them with `ctrl` on Windows and Linux or `command` on Mac, and then select `>`.
  - To select every workload the list shows, select `>>`.
  - To remove workloads from **Chosen Workloads**, select them and then select `<`. To remove all of them, select `<<`.

  The workloads you move appear in **Chosen Workloads**.

  </DocStep>
  <DocStep title="Fill in the remaining mandatory fields">

  For more information, refer to [How to configure Data Stream main settings](/en/documentation/guides/platform/observability/use-data-stream/).

  </DocStep>
  <DocStep title="Turn on Active">

  Make sure the **Active** switch is on.

  </DocStep>
  <DocStep title="Save the stream">

  Select **Save**.

  </DocStep>
</DocSteps>

The stream collects the events of the workloads you chose and sends them to its endpoint.
