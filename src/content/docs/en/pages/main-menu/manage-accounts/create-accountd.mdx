You can create an Azion account from the [sign-up page](https://console.azion.com/signup) with a work email or with [Social Login](/en/documentation/fundamentals/social-login/). Social Login accepts a GitHub or a Google account. The account is where every resource you create lives, and you reach it through [Azion Console](https://console.azion.com/). To add a person to an account that already exists, refer to [Users management](/en/documentation/fundamentals/users-management/).

---

## Sign up with a work email

To sign up with a work email:

<DocSteps>
  <DocStep title="Open the sign-up page">

  Access the [Azion Console sign-up page](https://console.azion.com/signup).

  </DocStep>
  <DocStep title="Select Sign Up with Work Email">

  The **Work Email** and **Password** fields open.

  </DocStep>
  <DocStep title="Enter your email and a password">

  In **Work Email**, enter the address for the account. In **Password**, enter the password for it.

  </DocStep>
  <DocStep title="Select Sign Up">

  When you select **Sign Up**, you accept the [Terms of Service](/en/documentation/agreements/customer-agreement/) and the [Privacy Policy](/en/documentation/agreements/privacy-policy/).

  </DocStep>
</DocSteps>

Azion sends a verification email to the address you entered. The link in it is valid for 24 hours.

:::note
If the email is not in your inbox, check the *Spam* folder. For help with the sign-up, [contact Azion](https://www.azion.com/en/contact/).
:::

To confirm the address and sign in:

<DocSteps>
  <DocStep title="Select the link in the verification email">

  The link activates the account and opens [Azion Console](https://console.azion.com/).

  </DocStep>
  <DocStep title="Enter your email and select Next" />
  <DocStep title="Enter your password and select Sign In" />
  <DocStep title="Complete the form that personalizes your experience" />
  <DocStep title="Select Submit" />
</DocSteps>

Azion Console opens on its home page, and the account is ready for its first resources.

---

## Sign up with a GitHub or Google account

With Social Login, the provider has already validated your email, so Azion sends no verification email. To sign up with a GitHub or a Google account:

<DocSteps>
  <DocStep title="Open the sign-up page">

  Access the [Azion Console sign-up page](https://console.azion.com/signup).

  </DocStep>
  <DocStep title="Select Continue with GitHub or Continue with Google" />
  <DocStep title="Sign in to the provider">

  If the provider asks, enter the username and password of that account and authorize the access.

  </DocStep>
  <DocStep title="Complete the form that personalizes your experience" />
  <DocStep title="Select Submit" />
</DocSteps>

Azion Console opens on its home page, and the account is ready for its first resources.
