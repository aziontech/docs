A security response team is a group of security specialists that you engage when an application is under attack or a security event unfolds. The team investigates the event, contains it, and adjusts your protections during the attack, so you do not diagnose and mitigate it alone.

**Security Response Team (SRT)** gives you on-request access to Azion security specialists, 24x7, for security response within the scope you contract. You engage the SRT through a Support ticket. The SRT reviews your security events and constructs customized security rules for your applications. Use the SRT to respond to an incident, tune your [Web Application Firewall (WAF)](/en/documentation/products/secure/firewall/web-application-firewall/), mitigate a DDoS attack, protect against bots, analyze a threat, apply an emergency change, or get critical support for a production environment.

---

## Request flow

Every engagement of the SRT starts with a Support ticket. [Technical Support](/en/documentation/services/support/) handles the ticket and every contact with you from start to finish, and the SRT performs the security work behind it. In an under-attack case, all communication with you happens through the ticket.

Support escalates a case to the SRT when the case needs deeper analysis, or when containing the attack goes beyond Support's break-and-fix scope. Typical escalations are attack or fraud response and solutions that require [Azion Marketplace](/en/documentation/products/marketplace/). The escalation is billable. Without the SRT contracted, it also requires the consent of your Customer Success Manager (CSM) or Account Executive (AE).

Before escalating, Support runs a pre-escalation review with you that covers:

- How [Firewall](/en/documentation/products/secure/firewall/) works, including the use cases for [Rules Engine](/en/documentation/products/secure/firewall/rules-engine/), Rate Limit, and [Network Lists](/en/documentation/products/secure/edge-firewall/network-layer-protection/network-lists/).
- WAF best practices, such as keeping the WAF in Learning mode and without exceptions.
- Additional services available in Azion Marketplace.
- Automations with the [Azion API](/en/documentation/products/deploy/automate/api/).
- Detailed request data from the [GraphQL API](/en/documentation/devtools/graphql-api/overview/).
- Confirmation that no penetration test or load test is running.
- The reference documentation for your case.
- Confirmation that you want the Security team engaged.

The escalation follows these steps:

1. You open a Support ticket that describes the attack or the security event.
2. Support reproduces the case, collects the evidence, and runs the pre-escalation review with you.
3. Support confirms whether you have the SRT contracted. With the SRT contracted, Support opens the case with the SRT and engages it directly.
4. Without the SRT contracted, the SRT aligns the next steps with your CSM. When the SRT cannot reach your CSM or AE, for example outside business hours, it proceeds with the service. Support informs you that the case is handled as an exception, and the SRT aligns it with your CSM or AE afterward.
5. Support updates the ticket at least once per hour with the status of the investigation, even for a low-impact case.

## Request contents

Support needs the following information to reproduce the case and escalate it to the SRT:

- The domain and the route (URI) under attack, with the affected URLs.
- The source IP addresses of the attack.
- Whether your own monitoring detected the attack, with any data from it that helps the investigation.
- Whether the application is unavailable.
- Evidence of the event: logs, screenshots, and steps to reproduce.
- A brief summary of the context.

## Service level

An issue handled by the SRT carries a 2-hour service level agreement (SLA) and 24x7 work time. Azion controls that SLA through the Support ticket associated with the incident, so every SRT engagement has one.

## Scope

The SRT covers the following activities within the contracted scope.

- **Incident response**: The SRT leads the response to a security incident on your applications. It reviews the security events, investigates the attack, and guides the mitigation during or after the event.
- **Security operations**: The SRT runs the security operations that the contracted scope covers, in coordination with Support and other Azion teams.
- **WAF tuning**: The SRT tunes your WAF and constructs customized security rules for your applications.
- **DDoS mitigation**: [DDoS Protection](/en/documentation/products/secure/firewall/ddos-protection/) detects and blocks attacks automatically. The SRT supports you during or after a DDoS attack, tracks the threat end to end, and applies customized rules against sophisticated attacks.
- **Bot protection**: The SRT responds to malicious automated traffic against your applications and to fraud attempts, and constructs the rules that block it. For more information, refer to [Bot Manager](/en/documentation/products/secure/firewall/bot-manager/).
- **Threat analysis**: The SRT analyzes the threats that reach your applications. It reviews the security events, determines the root cause, and recommends the mitigation.
- **Emergency change management**: A critical incident may require the immediate engagement of the SRT to change your configuration. A planned change follows the standard change management process instead. That process includes technical validation, rollback planning, validation in preview or staging when applicable, controlled production deployment, and least-privilege access throughout the execution.
- **Critical production support**: The SRT provides critical support for the production environments of enterprise customers, 24x7, within the contracted scope.

## Contract and hours

The SRT is a paid add-on, sold as a monthly package of contracted hours. The hours are valid during the monthly period, up to the monthly limit in your contract. Unused hours do not roll over to the next month. Azion bills the SRT by the number of packages you contract. The number of hours per package is on the [Pricing](/en/documentation/products/pricing/#professional-services) page.

Without the SRT contracted, Support can still engage the SRT as a billable exception. The SRT then aligns the next steps with your CSM or AE. To contract the SRT, contact [Sales](https://www.azion.com/en/contact/) or your Customer Success team.

## Responsibilities

- **Your security**: You remain responsible for the security of your applications, origins, configurations, access controls, data, and systems. You also decide whether to implement each recommendation or configuration the SRT proposes.
- **No absolute guarantee**: Azion does not guarantee that every attack, threat, vulnerability, or incident is prevented, detected, mitigated, or resolved. The SRT complements the protections you configure and does not replace them.
- **Actions at your request**: An action the SRT takes at your request or with your approval can affect the availability or performance of your applications. Azion is not responsible for that effect. The same applies when you delay an approval, provide incomplete information, or change your configuration outside the agreed scope. It also applies when the effect comes from third-party services or from an unavailable origin.
- **Support ownership**: The SRT does not replace Technical Support. Support handles the ticket from start to finish and is your point of contact during the engagement.
